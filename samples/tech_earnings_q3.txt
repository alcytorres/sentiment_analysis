Apex Technologies Inc. — Q3 2025 Earnings Summary

Revenue for Q3 2025 came in at $4.2 billion, up 18% year-over-year and ahead of analyst consensus estimates of $3.95 billion. The beat was driven primarily by strong demand in the cloud infrastructure segment, which grew 27% to $1.8 billion. Management raised full-year guidance to $16.5–$17.0 billion, reflecting confidence in the product pipeline and enterprise adoption trends.

Gross margins expanded 120 basis points to 62.4%, supported by favorable product mix and improved supply chain efficiency. Operating expenses grew 9%, well below revenue growth, resulting in significant operating leverage. Free cash flow reached $1.1 billion for the quarter, with the company announcing a $2 billion share buyback program.

CEO Lisa Park noted on the earnings call: "We are seeing accelerating demand across our AI-enabled product suite. Enterprise customers are consolidating their infrastructure spend with fewer vendors, and we are winning those decisions at a higher rate than any period in our history."

The company added 340 new enterprise customers during the quarter, bringing total enterprise accounts to 4,800. Net revenue retention rate held steady at 128%, indicating strong expansion within existing accounts. International revenue grew 22%, with particular strength in the APAC region.

Management flagged some caution around macroeconomic uncertainty in Europe and potential FX headwinds in Q4, but maintained that the demand environment remains robust. They expect operating margins to continue expanding through fiscal 2026 as scale benefits compound.
